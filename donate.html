Turkey Emergency Relief Mission

Families across Balıkesir and the surrounding region are facing an immediate and urgent need for food, clean water, emergency shelter, and medical assistance. Our teams are on the ground now — every gift, of any size, helps us reach the next family faster.

Why This Mission

On the ground, close to the need.

When emergencies hit, the gap between a donation and a delivered blanket, meal, or medical kit is what matters most. Our response teams are working directly in and around Balıkesir to identify the families who need help first — the displaced, the elderly, and households with young children.

Four needs are driving this mission right now: emergency food supplies, clean drinking water, temporary shelter materials, and basic medical assistance for people who can't otherwise reach a clinic. Funds raised through this appeal go directly toward those four categories.

Where Your Gift Goes

  • Food & Clean Water

    Emergency supplies for families with no other access.

  • Emergency Shelter

    Temporary shelter materials for displaced households.

  • Medical Assistance

    Basic care and supplies for those who can't reach a clinic.
